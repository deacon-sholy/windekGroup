About Windek Group

Nigeria's Integrated Industrial Powerhouse

Windek Group is a diversified Nigerian conglomerate delivering end-to-end industrial solutions that power national development. We operate across three high-impact verticals: Energy Infrastructure, Logistics & Supply Chain, and Fish Distribution.

Founded on the principles of excellence and innovation, Windek Group has grown to become a trusted partner for government agencies, multinational corporations, and development organizations across Nigeria and West Africa.

Our Vision

To be West Africa's most trusted and diversified industrial conglomerate, driving sustainable development through world-class infrastructure and services.

Our Mission

Delivering integrated industrial solutions that accelerate national growth, empower communities, and create long-term stakeholder value.
