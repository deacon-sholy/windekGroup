Compliance

Windek Group is committed to operating with the highest standards of regulatory, ethical, environmental, and safety compliance across our businesses.

1. Regulatory Commitment

We comply with applicable Nigerian laws, industry regulations, and licensing requirements, including standards set by federal agencies and energy sector regulators.

2. Health, Safety & Environment

Health, safety and environmental stewardship are essential to our operations. We maintain policies and procedures to protect employees, contractors, partners, and communities.

3. Quality and Risk Management

Windek Group applies strong quality controls and risk management practices throughout project delivery, logistics and supply chain management.

4. Anti-Corruption and Ethics

We uphold zero tolerance for bribery, corruption, fraud, or unethical conduct. Our team adheres to ethical business behavior and transparent decision-making.

5. Data Protection

We protect personal and corporate information through strong privacy, security, and access control practices. Our data handling aligns with our Privacy Policy.

6. Supplier and Partner Standards

We expect partners and suppliers to meet our compliance standards for safety, quality, environmental responsibility and ethical conduct.

7. Audits and Reporting

We conduct regular audits, monitoring, and reporting to ensure compliance with internal policies and external regulations.

8. Continuous Improvement

Our compliance programme is continuously reviewed and improved to reflect new regulations, best practices and evolving stakeholder needs.

9. Contact

For compliance enquiries or to report concerns, contact us at info@windekgroup.com or call 0808 526 9328.
